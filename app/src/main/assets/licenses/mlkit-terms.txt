Google ML Kit Terms of Service
================================

Serifu uses Google ML Kit on-device APIs for:
1. Text Recognition v2 (Japanese, Chinese, and Korean bundled models).
2. On-Device Translation (offline neural translation models downloaded on-device).

Terms of Service:
https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

All ML Kit processing runs entirely on your device. Image frames and
translated strings are never sent to Google servers by Serifu.
Downloaded language packs are stored locally on the device in private app storage.
